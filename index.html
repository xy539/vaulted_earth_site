About Our Vaulted Earth

Our Vaulted Earth is a Hindi-language documentary YouTube channel dedicated to exploring the ancient mysteries, forbidden places, hidden histories, and cosmic wonders of our world — with a focus on Indian civilisation, Vedic heritage, and archaeological discoveries that mainstream media overlooks.

Each episode is deeply researched, narrated in pure Hindi, and produced to the standard of a premium documentary — intelligent, restrained, and genuinely curious.

What We Cover

  • Ancient Indian temples and archaeological sites
  • Vedic and Puranic connections to physical evidence
  • Forbidden and inaccessible places worldwide
  • Space and cosmic mysteries
  • Suppressed or overlooked historical discoveries

YouTube Integration

This application uses the YouTube Data API v3 solely to upload and manage our own channel's video content. We do not access, read, or modify any other user's YouTube data.

Specifically, the application:

  • Uploads videos to our own YouTube channel
  • Sets video privacy status (private/public)
  • Updates video metadata (title, description, tags)

The application does not collect, store, or share any viewer data. It operates solely on behalf of the channel owner.

Privacy Policy

Effective: September 2026

Data We Collect

Our Vaulted Earth does not collect personal data from viewers or users. This application is an internal content production tool used exclusively by the channel owner.

YouTube API Usage

We use the YouTube Data API v3 to upload videos to our own YouTube channel. OAuth 2.0 credentials are stored securely on our private server and are never shared with third parties.

Our use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

OAuth Token Storage

OAuth tokens are stored locally on our private server in an encrypted credentials file. Tokens are used only to authenticate API requests to upload content to our own channel. We do not transmit tokens to any external service.

Revoking Access

You can revoke our application's access to your Google account at any time by visiting Google Account Permissions .

Third-Party Services

Our content production pipeline uses the following services:

  • Anthropic Claude API — script writing and research
  • ElevenLabs — Hindi voiceover generation
  • Replicate — image generation
  • Suno (via TTAPI) — background music generation
  • Google Sheets API — internal production tracking
  • YouTube Data API v3 — video upload and management

None of these services receive viewer personal data. All data processed is internal production content.
